Commercial terms

Rent or buy

Every product in this catalogue is available both ways. Which one is right depends almost entirely on how long the equipment will be needed, and that is usually knowable at the point of discharge.

Two ways to get the same equipment

Both options, side by side

Rent

When the need has an end date

Post-operative recovery, a fracture, a chest infection, or a hospital discharge where nobody yet knows how long care will be needed. Rental costs a fraction of purchase and ends when the need does.

  • Monthly terms on cots, wheelchairs, mattresses, concentrators, CPAP and BiPAP
  • Delivery, installation and collection included
  • Servicing included for the whole rental period
  • Deposit adjusted against purchase if the need becomes permanent
  • Extendable month to month, with no fresh delivery charge
Buy

When therapy is for the long term

Sleep apnea, COPD, and any prescription expected to run for years. Beyond roughly eight to twelve months of continuous use, ownership is normally the cheaper route, and the machine is configured to one patient.

  • Manufacturer warranty registered in the patient's name
  • Installation and training included
  • AMC and CMC contracts available from year two
  • Consumables supplied on a replacement schedule
  • GST invoice for insurance and institutional claims
